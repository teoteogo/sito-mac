The PV System

A complete pharmacovigilance system, run for you

For three marketing authorisation holders we are the entire pharmacovigilance system. Legal responsibility stays with the marketing authorisation holder (MAH), as the law requires. Who does what is written into the contract and the pharmacovigilance system master file (PSMF). Coverage

Your obligations, and how we cover them

# What the MAH must have How we cover it
01 A qualified person, resident and operating in the EU/EEA. We provide the EU-QPPV and a deputy, for marketing authorisation holders in Italy, Germany and Switzerland, and the local pharmacovigilance contact for Italy.
02 A PSMF that describes the system as it is. We write it, keep it current and record deviations in it.
03 Serious cases submitted within 15 days, non-serious within 90. Intake, validity check, duplicate search, MedDRA coding, medical assessment, follow-up and E2B(R3) submission to EudraVigilance.
04 Medical literature monitored every week. Weekly searches in PubMed and Embase, and screening of the EMA medical literature monitoring (MLM) output.
05 Periodic safety update reports and risk management plans. More than 50 aggregate reports a year for EU and non-EU authorities; PSURs in PBRER format.
06 Signals detected, validated and documented. More than 100 signal detection sessions a year in EVDAS, with medical review.
07 Product data kept up to date. Article 57 data in XEVMPD, and the transition to the EMA Product Management Service (PMS).
08 Safety variations and authority requests. Updates to the summary of product characteristics (SmPC) and package leaflet, and replies to EMA and AIFA requests.
09 A quality system that is audited. Documented procedures, compliance indicators and risk-based audits that include our own subcontractors.
Governance

Who does what

You keep the decisions that belong to the MAH. We run the operations and report on them.

The contract and the safety data exchange agreement (SDEA) set out roles, data flows, timelines and your right to audit us. You receive compliance indicators at the frequency we agree, and you always know who your contacts are.

Transition

Moving your system to MAC

1

Gap assessment and plan

We review your current system and agree a documented transition plan with dates.

2

Agreements, QPPV and PSMF

Contract and SDEA signed, QPPV change notified, PSMF written or transferred.

3

Data migration

Cases and product data move into the validated database, and the migration is verified.

4

We take over operations and review the first weeks with you.

Regulation

What Regulation (EU) 2025/1466 asks of outsourced pharmacovigilance

Since 12 February 2026:

  • Subcontracts must define roles, data exchange and audit rights.
  • A subcontractor cannot pass work on without written consent.
  • Major and critical deviations go into the PSMF.
  • Risk-based audits cover every pharmacovigilance activity, vendors included.

Our contracts and procedures are built on these requirements Bozza da confermare: adeguamento effettivo di contratti e procedure al Reg. (UE) 2025/1466; testo del regolamento da rileggere sulla fonte ufficiale.
