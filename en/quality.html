Ethics & Compliance

Quality and Ethics Code

We operate with integrity, transparency and rigour in every activity. The principles guiding our work are not mere statements of intent: they are the concrete foundation of every professional relationship we build.

Our founding principles

Quality across four dimensions

Quality

Methodological rigour, technical expertise and a focus on delivering real value to the client in every advisory activity.

Accuracy

Analyses, assessments and recommendations based exclusively on reliable, verifiable data.

Traceability

Clear, reconstructible and auditable processes and documentation. Every decision is justified and documented.

Accountability

Full awareness of the impact of our activities on clients and on the pharmaceutical sector.

Ethics Code

The principles that guide our work

An ethics code is not a document: it is how we operate every day, in every professional relationship, with clients, partners and institutions.

01

Regulatory compliance

We uphold and promote Good Practices (GxP) and national and international sector regulations, supporting clients in meeting every regulatory obligation.

02

Integrity and anti-corruption

Every relationship is managed ethically, is documented and remains fully compliant. We do not tolerate conflicts of interest, favouritism or any practice that could compromise our credibility.

03

Professional independence

We operate independently from our clients, always safeguarding the objectivity of our analyses and recommendations. Independence is the core value of our advisory work.

04

Client relationships

Collaboration, reliability and mutual accountability: we act as a professional partner. Implementation of our recommendations and regulatory compliance remain the client's responsibility.

05

Confidentiality and GDPR

We protect clients' technical, scientific and commercial information with the utmost confidentiality, in full compliance with the European GDPR Regulation.

06

Partners and suppliers

We build partnerships founded on legality, quality and mutual respect, with the aim of creating a network that shares high professional and ethical standards across the pharmaceutical sector.

Recognitions and compliance

Our Quality Management System (QMS) is structured to ensure regulatory compliance, full traceability and data security. We operate in accordance with Good Pharmacovigilance Practices (GVP) and applicable European and national directives, underpinned by a rigorous and robust procedural framework.

Our core Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Below are the most significant procedural pillars that govern our daily activities and ensure data quality and regulatory compliance:

  • Personnel Management and Continuous Training
  • Case Management and Processing
  • Literature Surveillance
  • IT System Validation (GAMP 5 compliant)
  • Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (24/7 availability)
  • Personal Data Protection (GDPR)
  • Compliance Control and Management Review
  • Change Management (Change Process)
  • Drafting and Implementation of Risk Management Plans (RMP)
