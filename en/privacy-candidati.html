Recruitment Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of candidates’ personal data pursuant to Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

This is an English translation provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

Medical Affairs Consulting Srl
Corso Italia, 39 – 21047 Saronno (VA), Italy
VAT No.: 04027560129
Email: privacy@med-af-consulting.it

2. Subject matter and nature of the processing

Pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) (EU) 2016/679, MAC Srl, with registered office in Corso Italia 39, 21047 Saronno (VA), VAT No. 04027560129, represented by its pro-tempore legal representative, as Data Controller, wishes to inform you about the processing of your personal data for recruitment purposes, provided by you directly in response to advertisements or staff searches, or by sending your curriculum vitae, or otherwise received.

Your data will be processed in full compliance with the applicable legislation and only for the purposes and in the ways set out below. The personal data contained in your CV (personal details, email and telephone contacts, photograph, tax code, professional data, salary and company classification data, data relating to training and education, interests) or otherwise provided by you will be processed.

Data and information that you have freely made public through your public social media profiles may also be processed; such information may become known to the Controller within the limits of the principles of lawfulness and relevance and will be deleted at the end of the selection phase.

Special categories of data under Article 9 GDPR are not requested, but if your CV spontaneously contains such data (for example, data capable of revealing membership of protected categories), the Controller will process only the data relevant to assessing your professional aptitude, to the extent that obtaining such information is strictly indispensable to establishing the employment or collaboration relationship.

Only the information strictly relevant and limited to what is necessary for the purpose indicated below will be processed, also taking into account the particular duties and/or the specific features of the professional profiles sought.

If the CVs sent contain data that is not relevant to the purpose pursued, the Controller will refrain from using that information.

3. Methods of the processing

The processing will comply with the obligations of fairness, lawfulness, transparency and protection of confidentiality and integrity, in accordance with the principles and provisions of the above-mentioned personal data protection legislation.

In particular, the data processed will be strictly relevant to the purposes specified below and to the related obligations and tasks, and which cannot be fulfilled or achieved through the processing of anonymous data or data of a different nature.

Your personal data will be processed on paper and by electronic means and will be protected by stringent security measures to guarantee its security and confidentiality. Access to your data will be allowed only to the persons involved in the evaluation and selection process.

The processing will be carried out by duly authorised personnel in compliance with, and applying, the security measures suitable to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity and completeness of the data processed, as required by Article 32 GDPR and in accordance with the instructions given by the Data Controller.

4. Purposes of the processing

Your data will be processed for the following purposes:

  • carrying out the necessary assessments for the possible establishment of an employment or collaboration relationship;
  • in the event of a positive assessment, preparing the documentation needed to establish the employment or collaboration relationship.

For the processing of your personal data through a response to an advertisement or staff search, other recruitment portals and channels, or professional social media profiles (e.g. LinkedIn), the legal basis adopted is the performance of pre-contractual measures taken at the request of the data subject (Article 6(1)(b)) and, for CVs sent spontaneously, Article 111-bis of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003.

Any special categories of data contained in your CV, if spontaneously included by you, will be processed exclusively to fulfil specific obligations or to perform specific tasks provided for by labour and social security law (Article 9(2)(b)) and in accordance with the legal requirements on the processing of special categories of data carried out in the pre-employment phase.

No automated decision-making, including profiling as referred to in Article 22(1) and (4) of the Regulation, is carried out on your personal data.

5. Nature of the data provision

Providing data for the stated purpose is optional but necessary, and is an essential requirement for achieving the purpose described. Failure to provide your data will make it impossible to assess your profile and professional aptitude for the role to be filled or for any future positions that may become available, and will not allow us to proceed with the selection activities for the possible establishment of an employment relationship.

6. Retention period

Data communicated by sending the CV (personal data, contact data, education data, professional history data, interests, desired occupation, and any data relating to health or membership of protected categories) is kept for no longer than 12 months (starting from receipt of the CV) to assess the company’s staff recruitment needs, unless an employment relationship is entered into, in which case the data will be kept for the time related to it.

If you are hired, the Controller will provide you with a separate privacy notice informing you about how your personal data is stored and used during your employment.

7. Access to data and disclosure

Your personal data may be processed by the Controller and by the persons authorised by the Controller who need to process it in the performance of their duties, duly instructed to comply with personal data protection principles.

Your data may also be disclosed to all parties entitled to access it in compliance with legal provisions.

In particular, your data may be disclosed, including at the pre-contractual stage, to third-party companies or professionals (both natural and legal persons) to whom the Controller may entrust tasks for the performance of certain outsourced activities and who are duly appointed as Data Processors pursuant to Article 28 of the EU Regulation (e.g. staff selection officers, employment consultants preparing the documentation needed for the possible establishment of the employment relationship, web agencies in charge of managing the website, etc.).

Disclosure of your data to the above categories of parties is necessary to carry out the preliminary steps required before any employment relationship is established.

The complete list of Processors and third-party companies to which your data is disclosed is available at our registered office. The complete and updated list of the parties to whom your personal data may be disclosed can be requested by contacting the Data Controller.

Your personal data is not disseminated.

8. Transfer of data outside the EU

Your data will not be transferred outside the territory of the EU.

Should the data be transferred to third countries (non-EU), including countries that may not guarantee the same level of protection as the applicable privacy legislation, the Controller informs you that the processing will in any case take place in one of the ways permitted by the Regulation, such as your consent, the adoption of Standard Clauses approved by the European Commission, the possible selection of parties participating in international data-flow programmes, or operating in countries considered safe by the European Commission.

9. Exercising your rights

You may at any time exercise the rights recognised by Articles 15-22 of Regulation (EU) 679/2016 (GDPR).

In particular, where the requirements are met, you may exercise the right of access to your data; you may also obtain the erasure, anonymisation or blocking of data processed in breach of the law, the updating, rectification or, where of interest, the integration of the data, or make use of data portability; you may also object, on legitimate grounds, to the processing of your data.

You also have the right to withdraw your consent to the processing at any time, where consent is required and has been given.

10. Complaints

If you believe that these rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority or another competent authority.
