Cookie Policy

Information on the use of cookies and similar technologies pursuant to the guidelines of the Italian Data Protection Authority and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Cookie Policy of Medical Affairs Consulting Srl — website www.med-af-consulting.com

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your computer or mobile device and that the website can read back later. Other browser storage technologies (such as local storage) work in a similar way and are treated in the same way in this policy.

Technical cookies are needed for the site to work or to remember your choices and do not require consent. Profiling cookies and third-party analytics cookies require your prior consent.

What this site uses

This site uses technical tools only. It does not use profiling cookies, third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or embedded maps or videos.

  • Consent management (Avacy) — stores in your browser the choice you made on the cookie banner (a technical item named oil_data), so that the banner is not shown at every visit and your choice can be documented. Provider: Avacy S.r.l. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • Display preference (mac_doodle_seen) — stored in your browser's local storage, it remembers that you have already seen our 25th-anniversary message so that it is not shown again. It contains no personal data and stays on your device until you delete it from your browser.

The site's fonts are served directly from our own server: they are not requested from external services and no IP address is transmitted to third parties for this purpose.

Third-party services

The site contains simple links to our LinkedIn and Instagram profiles and to content published there. These links do not load any script from those platforms until you select them: only after you click do you leave this site, and the privacy and cookie policies of the platform you visit apply.

How to manage your preferences

You can reopen the preferences panel at any time using the Cookie preferences link in the page footer.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings:
