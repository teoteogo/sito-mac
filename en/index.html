About us

Alongside pharmaceutical companies since 2001

Medical Affairs Consulting (MAC) was founded in 2001 by Dr. Stefano Franchini, a physician and surgeon specialised in Sports Medicine and formerly on staff at the IRCCS Don Gnocchi Institute in Milan. Drawing on an established career as Medical Advisor and Drug Safety Unit Manager at Zambon Group SpA, Dr. Franchini brought to MAC an in-depth understanding of industry dynamics and regulatory practice.

Discover our expertise

Founded to address the needs of pharmaceutical companies with scientific rigour, MAC has grown steadily on the strength of the market's trust.

Today the company comprises a multidisciplinary team of more than 25 specialists, offering pharmaceutical companies a scalable structure, deep vertical expertise and the same commitment to excellence that has guided us since day one.

Physicians, pharmacists, biologists, engineers, IT specialists, quality managers and other professionals work side by side every day, each with a distinct role but a shared objective. It is precisely this convergence of skills, backgrounds and individual expertise that allows us to address the complexity of the pharmaceutical sector effectively — and to respond promptly to every regulatory, operational or market change.

Our services

Comprehensive support across the drug lifecycle

From pharmacovigilance management to medical information activities: we deliver integrated, specialist support.

Pharmacovigilance

We manage every aspect of drug safety across the full product lifecycle, with an approach centred on quality and regulatory compliance.

  • EU-QPPV and PSMF — full regulatory oversight
  • ICSRs, SAEs, SUSARs and Eudravigilance
  • Periodic safety reports — PSUR / PBRER
  • Signal detection and benefit-risk profile analysis
  • Internal audits and gap assessments of PV processes

Medical Affairs

We support the company's medical-scientific activities through a structured, traceable operating model geared towards performance improvement.

  • Medical Information — Legislative Decree 219/2006, Art. 126
  • Support to the Scientific Service Officer
  • Management and analysis of medical enquiries
  • Scientific exchange and KOL engagement
  • Review of scientific materials and medical team training

THE M.A.C. METHOD

Quality is not by chance.
It is a method.

Every result we deliver for our clients stems from a structured, replicable and continuously evolving approach.
Discover the three pillars that guide our daily work, and the practical examples that show how we translate this value into practice.

  1. 01

    People at the Centre

    We cultivate high-calibre professionals, integrating their talent into a shared method and creating the conditions for them to realise their full potential. We believe excellence is not a starting point, but a value we build together, every day, as a team.

    Our day begins with the Asaichi, a daily alignment ritual that enables us to promptly resolve the complexities carried over from the previous day, putting every professional in the best position to perform at their highest level.
  2. 02

    Rigorous Processes

    We operate according to documented standards and industry best practice. Every activity is traceable, and every decision is underpinned by a clear process — because our clients require certainty.

    In practice, we have translated this rigour into a visual management system built on digital dashboards, displaying the priorities and timelines of every submission in real time. This allows us to distribute workloads efficiently and in a balanced manner, instantly aligning every stakeholder involved so that no deadline is ever left to chance.
  3. 03

    Continuous Improvement

    Every process is a starting point. We continuously analyse, measure and optimise — because in pharmacovigilance, yesterday's best is never enough for tomorrow.

    In practice, we sustain an ongoing process that gathers improvement ideas from the team on a daily basis. This enables us to implement more than 300 Kaizen optimisations every year, continuously raising the quality standards of our processes.
