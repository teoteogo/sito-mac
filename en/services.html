Pharmacovigilance

We provide comprehensive, fully outsourced management of the pharmacovigilance system for Marketing Authorisation Holders and Sponsors. We deploy a solid organisational structure, proven safety procedures and validated databases, ensuring full compliance with GVP (Good Pharmacovigilance Practices) guidelines and a continuous state of inspection-readiness. Our safety system has already undergone regulatory authority (AIFA) inspections with zero critical findings.

EU-QPPV & Deputy QPPV
Role coverage at both European and Italian level, with round-the-clock availability
Case Processing
Triage, data entry/quality control, MedDRA coding, medical assessment and submission of ICSRs/SUSARs
Literature Search
Periodic, rigorous monitoring of global and local (AIFA) scientific literature
Signal Detection
Signal detection and management for continuous monitoring of the benefit-risk profile
PSMF
Structuring, drafting and maintenance of the Pharmacovigilance System Master File
Safety Database
Use of a validated safety database (ICH E2B R3) compliant with GVP and GAMP 5
Governance & SOPs
Processes governed by dedicated Standard Operating Procedures

Periodic Reports

Drafting of periodic safety reports and management of submissions to EMA and AIFA, in full compliance with regulatory deadlines.

PSUR / PBRER and DSUR
Drafting and submission of periodic safety reports for marketed products and those in clinical development
Addendum Clinical Overview
Preparation of the scientific AdCO in support of five-year marketing authorisation renewal procedures
Risk Management Plan
Drafting, updating and monitoring of risk minimisation measures (RMP)

Medical Affairs

A complete Medical Affairs service pursuant to Italian Legislative Decree 219/2006, Art. 126.

Scientific Service
Full support and advisory services to the Scientific Service Officer under Art. 126 of Legislative Decree 219/06
Medical Information
Qualified, timely and fully traceable management of scientific/medical enquiries from physicians, pharmacists and patients
Standard Response
Preparation and maintenance of Standard Response Letters to streamline response workflows
Scientific Materials
Review and approval of scientific materials and related publications

Quality & Compliance

Support for a documented, audit-ready pharmacovigilance system: procedures, gap analyses and assistance during GVP audits and inspections.

Audits & Inspections
Support in preparing for regulatory inspections (AIFA/EMA) and conducting internal audits of the quality system
SOP & QMS
Drafting, review, structuring and implementation of Standard Operating Procedures and quality management systems
Gap Analysis
Comprehensive assessment of pharmacovigilance processes to identify areas for improvement and define CAPAs

Materials & Training

Scientific review and regulatory compliance of promotional materials, with dedicated training programmes for pharmaceutical sales representatives.

Promotional Materials
Scientific and compliance certification under Art. 113 of Legislative Decree 219/06 for promotional materials, brochures and digital channels
AIFA Filing
Support with notification and filing procedures for promotional materials with the regulatory authority
Sales Representative Training
Dedicated regulatory and scientific training for pharmaceutical sales representatives

Lean Consulting

Our expertise

Four souls, one team

The quality of our services stems from the combination of diverse, complementary expertise working together every day.

Medical-scientific expertise

Physicians, pharmacists and biologists with proven experience in pharmacovigilance and medical affairs. They manage safety databases, literature searches, signal detection and PSUR drafting, ensuring scientific rigour in every assessment.

Lean methodology and process engineering

Lean Thinking and Kaizen form the methodological foundation of all our processes. Elimination of non-value-adding activities, measurability, standardisation and continuous improvement, applied across the board.

Quality system and compliance

SOP drafting, document management and staff training ensure compliance with the GVP Modules, EU Regulation 2025/1466 and ISO 9001 standards, both in our own processes and in those of our clients.

IT infrastructure and digital tools

Microsoft 365 and SharePoint support collaboration and document traceability, within environments compliant with pharmaceutical industry requirements and GxP best practice.
