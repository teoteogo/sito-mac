The services

Complete support, from safety to quality

Six areas of intervention, one partner. The essentials for each service are below; technical details open on request.

Pharmacovigilance

Complete management of the pharmacovigilance system for MAH holders in the European Union, from EU-QPPV appointment to ICSR submission to EudraVigilance.

  • EU-QPPV and Deputy QPPV
  • ICSRs / SAEs / SUSARs, from triage to submission
  • PSMF and EudraVigilance
Technical details
  • ICSRs / SAEs / SUSARs — collection, data entry, MedDRA coding and medical review on the ArisGlobal LifeSphere platform
  • PSMF — drafting, structuring and maintenance of the Pharmacovigilance System Master File
  • EudraVigilance — registration and management of the European safety reporting system
  • XEVMPD / Art. 57 — management of medicinal product data in the Extended EudraVigilance dictionary

Periodic reports

Drafting of periodic safety reports and management of submissions to EMA and AIFA, in full compliance with regulatory deadlines.

  • PSUR / PBRER and DSUR
  • Signal detection and management
  • Risk Management Plan
Technical details
  • PSUR / PBRER — complete drafting with literature analysis and benefit-risk evaluation
  • DSUR — Development Safety Update Reports for medicines in clinical development
  • Signal detection — detection of safety signals per GVP Module IX
  • RMP — drafting, implementation and updates in line with EMA requirements
  • EMA / AIFA submissions — managed within the regulatory calendar deadlines

Medical Information

A complete medical information service pursuant to Italian Legislative Decree 219/2006 art. 126: from responses to HCP enquiries to management of the MI database.

  • Scientific Service Officer
  • Enquiry management and standard response letters
  • Literature searches
Technical details
  • Scientific Service Officer — coverage of the mandatory role under Legislative Decree 219/2006 art. 126
  • MI enquiries — accurate responses to HCPs, pharmacists and patients, with full traceability
  • Standard Response Letters — standardised letters for frequently asked questions
  • MI database — implementation and management for enquiry registration and traceability

Quality & Compliance

Support for a documented, audit-ready pharmacovigilance system: procedures, gap analysis and assistance during GVP audits and inspections.

  • SOP drafting and review
  • Gap analysis and inspection readiness
  • GVP staff training
Technical details
  • SOPs — drafting, review and updates aligned with the GVP Modules and EU Reg. 2025/1466
  • Gap analysis — PV system assessment, identification of non-conformities and remediation plan
  • GVP audits and inspections — document preparation, internal simulations, assistance during AIFA/EMA inspections and post-audit CAPAs
  • PV contracts — review of subcontractor agreements per EU Reg. 2025/1466 clauses

Materials & Training

Scientific review and regulatory compliance of promotional materials, with dedicated training programmes for pharmaceutical sales representatives.

  • Sales rep training on PV and regulations
  • Scientific review of materials
  • AIFA certification support
Technical details
  • Sales rep training — programmes on pharmacovigilance, ADR reporting and AIFA regulations, tailored per company
  • Materials review — verification of scientific accuracy before distribution to HCPs
  • Advertising compliance — verification pursuant to Legislative Decree 219/06 art. 113 ff.
  • Digital materials — review of digital content, websites and apps for healthcare professionals

Kaizen Consulting

Lean Thinking applied to pharmacovigilance processes and regulatory activities: less waste, shorter lead times and a system that scales with business growth.

  • Value Stream Mapping of PV processes
  • Kaizen Events and workshops
  • Pharmacovigilance KPIs
Technical details
  • Value Stream Mapping — mapping the PV workflow to identify bottlenecks and automation opportunities
  • Kaizen Events — intensive workshops (2–5 days) to implement rapid process improvements
  • KPIs — compliance rate, ICSR lead time, signal backlog, cost per case
  • Continuous improvement culture — training and coaching to make improvement a self-sustaining process
