Clients and Suppliers Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of clients’ and suppliers’ personal data pursuant to Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Subject matter and nature of the processing

Pursuant to Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), the Data Controller informs you that your data will be processed in full compliance with the applicable legislation and solely for the purposes set out below. The personal data that you provide when you are entrusted with an assignment relating to the professional activities carried out for the Controller will be processed.

They may include common data (first name, last name, address, telephone numbers, email), tax and payment data, data relating to your professional activity and career, and IP address data when you access company IT systems.

3. Purposes and methods of the processing

Your personal data is processed for the establishment of the collaboration relationship, as well as for the related regulatory obligations provided for by national or EU laws and/or regulations that the Controller is required to observe.

In particular, the Controller may process your personal data for:

  • a. Purposes connected with the establishment and performance of the professional collaboration relationship between you and the Controller, such as the formation of the contract and the performance of the tasks deriving from it, and the payment of fees.
    The legal basis adopted is the need to perform contractual obligations under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • b. Purposes connected with the protection, including in court, of the Controller’s assets, rights and interests and with the proper conduct of company activities in compliance with the rules in force, company policies and models aimed at preventing unlawful conduct.
    The legal basis adopted is the Controller’s legitimate interest in the proper formation and performance of the relationship, in fulfilling the obligations arising from it and, more generally, in protecting the company’s rights, processes and assets, pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The collection of data for the stated purposes is therefore necessary and is an essential requirement for the conclusion and performance of the contract. Without it, it would not be possible to start and carry out the relationship or otherwise proceed.

Where, during the relationship, further processing requiring your consent is envisaged, your consent will be requested on each occasion.

The processing of your personal information will be based on the principles of fairness, lawfulness and transparency and on the protection of your privacy and rights.

The processing will be carried out on electronic and paper media, and your data is collected and stored both in electronic databases and in paper archives.

The processing will be carried out by applying security measures suitable to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity and completeness of the data processed, as required by Article 32 GDPR and in accordance with the instructions given by the Data Controller.

4. Retention period

All of the above data will also be archived after the end of the collaboration relationship, for the fulfilment of any legal obligations connected with or arising from the termination of the relationship, for a period of 10 years, unless retention for a longer period is required by a legal obligation or is necessary to establish, exercise or defend a right of the Company in legal proceedings.

5. Access to data and disclosure

Your personal data may be processed by the Controller and by the persons authorised by the Controller who need to process it in the performance of their duties, duly instructed to comply with personal data protection principles.

Your data may also be disclosed to all parties entitled to access it in compliance with legal provisions.

In particular, your data may be disclosed to:

  • a. qualified parties that provide the Company with services instrumental to the purposes indicated above or otherwise related to the contractual relationship (e.g. banks, legal, tax, accounting and financial consultants);
  • b. other external suppliers providing services relating to the company’s business that are necessary or otherwise functional to the management of the relationship (e.g. IT service providers);
  • c. administrations and other public authorities in fulfilment of legal obligations.

The parties belonging to the above categories operate, in some cases, in full autonomy as separate Data Controllers and, in others, as Data Processors specifically appointed by the Controller pursuant to Article 28 GDPR. In this case, third parties may be selected from among those who, by experience, capability and reliability, offer adequate guarantees of full compliance with the provisions in force on data processing, including security.

The complete and updated list of the parties to whom your personal data may be disclosed can be requested by contacting the Controller.

Your personal data may be transferred to third parties outside the European Union and is not disseminated.

Should the data be transferred to third countries (non-EU), including countries that may not guarantee the same level of protection as the applicable privacy legislation, the Controller informs you that the processing will in any case take place in one of the ways permitted by the Regulation, such as your consent, the adoption of Standard Clauses approved by the European Commission, the selection of parties participating in international data-flow programmes (e.g. the EU-US Privacy Shield) or operating in countries considered safe by the European Commission.

6. Exercising your rights

You may at any time exercise the rights recognised by Articles 15-22 of Regulation (EU) 679/2016 (GDPR).

In particular, you may obtain from the above-mentioned Data Controller that your personal data be made available to you in intelligible form; you may also obtain the erasure, anonymisation or blocking of data processed in breach of the law, the updating, rectification or, where of interest, the integration of the data, or make use of data portability; you may also object, on legitimate grounds, to the processing of your data.

If you believe that these rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority or another competent authority.
