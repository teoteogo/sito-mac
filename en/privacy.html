2. Subject matter and nature of the processing

With your consent, the common personal data (first name, last name, email) that you provide by sending communications (email, telephone) to our contact points will be processed.

3. Purposes and methods of the processing

Your data is processed in order to handle your requests for information made through the Website or the other contact points made known by the Controller.

The legal basis for this purpose is the performance of pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Providing your personal and contact details is optional; however, if the data needed to reply (for example, a contact address) is missing, we will not be able to follow up on your request.

Your data may in any case be processed, even without your consent, in order to comply with obligations set by laws, regulations or EU legislation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) or for the Controller’s legitimate interest in defending and protecting its rights in legal proceedings (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The processing of your personal information will be based on the principles of fairness, lawfulness and transparency and on the protection of your privacy and rights.

The processing will be carried out on electronic and paper media, and your data is collected and stored both in electronic databases and in paper archives.

The processing will be carried out by applying security measures suitable to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity and completeness of the data processed, as required by Article 32 GDPR and in accordance with the instructions given by the Data Controller.

4. Retention period

Data will be kept for the period strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected; in any case, the criterion used to determine this period is based on compliance with the time limits allowed by applicable law and on the principles of data minimisation, storage limitation and rational management of archives.

5. Access to data and disclosure

Your personal data may be processed by the Controller and by the persons authorised by the Controller who need to process it in the performance of their duties, duly instructed to comply with personal data protection principles.

Your personal data will not be disseminated, but may be disclosed to all parties entitled to access it under legal provisions.

Data may be processed by third parties who need to know it because of their activity or duties, for the purposes described above (for example: IT service providers or providers managing the company website, consultants and suppliers providing commercial, professional and technical services related to the functions of the site). In this case, third parties may be selected from among those who, by experience, capability and reliability, offer adequate guarantees of full compliance with the provisions in force on data processing, including security.

These parties may be appointed as external Data Processors, and their updated list is available at any time by contacting the Controller.

The data you communicate to our contact points will be processed within the European territory. Should the data be transferred to third countries, including countries that may not guarantee the same level of protection as the applicable privacy legislation, the Controller informs you that the processing will in any case take place in one of the ways permitted by the Regulation, such as your consent, the adoption of Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, the selection of parties participating in international data-flow programmes (e.g. the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) or operating in countries considered safe by the European Commission.

7. Exercising your rights

You may at any time exercise the rights recognised by Articles 15-22 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 by contacting the Controller at the addresses indicated.

In particular, you may obtain from the Data Controller that your personal data be made available to you in intelligible form; you may also obtain the erasure, anonymisation or blocking of data processed in breach of the law, the updating, rectification or, where of interest, the integration of the data, or make use of data portability; you may also object, on legitimate grounds, to the processing.

If you believe that these rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it) or another competent authority.
