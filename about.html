2001–2026 · 25 years in pharmacovigilance

Where we come from

MAC was founded in 2001 by Dr Stefano Franchini, a physician specialised in sports medicine, formerly a medical executive at IRCCS Don Gnocchi and Drug Safety Unit Manager at Zambon Group.

The idea was simple: give pharmaceutical companies an outsourcing partner with the same scientific discipline they expect of themselves. The company has grown steadily since then, and our key clients have been with us for more than ten years.
