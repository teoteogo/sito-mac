Quality & Data

Quality and data protection

The guarantees behind the system: documented procedures, validated databases, and health data handled under clear rules.

Quality system

A quality system built on GVP

Written procedures

Standard operating procedures (SOPs) cover case management, literature monitoring, aggregate reports, signal management, vendor management, internal audits and corrective actions.

Checks against the source

Data entry is verified against the original documents, and the source of every case stays accessible.

Internal audits

Every year we audit our own compliance with EU good pharmacovigilance practices (GVP).

Trained people

Each role has a training plan and documented proof of competence. Training is updated whenever a regulation changes.

Improvement

How the system improves

A quality system is judged by what happens when something goes wrong or something changes. This is what happens here.

  1. 1

    We measure.

    Compliance indicators, such as on-time submissions, are tracked and reviewed with each client.

  2. 2

    We record deviations.

    A late case or a process error is logged, not explained away. Major and critical deviations go into the PSMF.

  3. 3

    We find the cause and fix it.

    Each deviation gets a root cause analysis and a corrective and preventive action (CAPA), with an owner and a due date.

  4. 4

    We check that the fix worked.

    Actions are closed only after their effectiveness is verified.

  5. 5

    We keep up with the rules.

    New guidance and regulations are assessed for impact and brought into procedures through change control.

Data

Security and data protection

Access by role and project

Access to each client project is granted by role and after project-specific training. Project authorisations are mapped.

Controlled access

VPN with multi-factor authentication.

Validated systems

Safety databases validated to GAMP 5 under a documented IT validation procedure.

Continuity

Automatic backups, a disaster recovery plan and on-call cover around the clock.

Our role under GDPR

We act as data processor for the marketing authorisation holder, under a written agreement (Article 28 GDPR). Product and commercial information is covered by confidentiality agreements.

Code of ethics

The principles behind every working relationship

01

Regulatory compliance

We uphold good practices (GxP) and national and international sector regulations, and support clients in meeting every regulatory obligation.

02

Integrity and anti-corruption

Every relationship is managed ethically and documented. We do not tolerate conflicts of interest, favouritism or any practice that could compromise our credibility.

03

Professional independence

Our analyses, assessments and recommendations are objective and based on reliable, verifiable data.

04

Client relationships

Legal responsibility stays with the marketing authorisation holder, as the law requires. Roles and responsibilities are defined in the contract and in the PSMF.

05

Confidentiality

We protect clients' technical, scientific and commercial information under confidentiality agreements and in line with the GDPR.

06

Partners and suppliers

We build partnerships on legality, quality and mutual respect, with partners who share high professional and ethical standards.
