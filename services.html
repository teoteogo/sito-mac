Pharmacovigilance

Pharmacovigilance modules

The full system All the modules below as one pharmacovigilance system, as we run it today for three marketing authorisation holders (MAHs).
The PV System
01

EU-QPPV and PSMF

Qualified person responsible for pharmacovigilance (EU-QPPV) and deputy; writing and maintenance of the pharmacovigilance system master file (PSMF).

02

Case management

Individual case safety reports (ICSRs) from intake to EudraVigilance submission, in SafetyDrugs® or ARISg™, E2B(R3). Submission to EMA and MHRA.

03

Medical assessment

Seriousness, causality and expectedness assessed by a drug safety physician.

04

Literature monitoring

Weekly searches in PubMed and Embase, MLM screening, case identification.

05

Aggregate reports

PSURs in PBRER format, risk management plans, addenda to clinical overviews.

06

Signal management

Detection in EVDAS, validation, assessment and documentation.

07

Clinical trial safety

For sponsors: management of suspected unexpected serious adverse reactions (SUSARs) and submission within 7 or 15 days.

08

Product data

XEVMPD maintenance and transition to PMS.

09

Safety variations and authority requests

SmPC and package leaflet updates; replies to EMA and AIFA.

10

Audits and gap assessments

Audits of your pharmacovigilance system and of your vendors; alignment of subcontracts with Regulation (EU) 2025/1466.

Medical Affairs

Medical Affairs: scientific support that stands apart from promotion

Italian law requires every marketing authorisation holder to run a scientific service that is independent of marketing. We run it with you, or for you, and keep every answer traceable.

Scientific service

Support to the person responsible for the scientific service under Article 126 of Legislative Decree 219/2006.

Medical information

Documented, referenced answers to enquiries from healthcare professionals and patients. Any adverse reaction mentioned in an enquiry goes straight to pharmacovigilance.

Review of promotional materials

Scientific review and regulatory conformity of materials for healthcare professionals (Articles 113–120 of Legislative Decree 219/2006).

Training

For medical and field teams: product, guidelines, and how to recognise and report an adverse reaction.
