Careers

Work in pharmacovigilance, with a team that trains you

We look for physicians, pharmacists, biologists and quality professionals who want to grow in drug safety.

Working at MAC

How you grow here

Structured GVP training

Each role has a training plan, with documented steps from first case to independent work.

A defined competency path

You know what is expected at each level, from data entry officer to senior drug safety officer.

Work that matters

Every case you handle contributes to the safe use of medicines.

Profiles

The roles on our team

Quality Assurance, pharmacovigilance (senior)

5+ years in pharmacovigilance QA. Excellent knowledge of EU/EEA pharmacovigilance legislation.

Medical Expert (senior)

Degree in medicine. 2+ years of medical activities in pharmacovigilance.

Drug Safety Officer (senior)

Pharmacist or biologist. 2+ years in pharmacovigilance.

Drug Safety Officer (junior)

Pharmacist or biologist. 6 months to 2 years in pharmacovigilance.

Data Entry Officer

Pharmacist or biologist. Up to 6 months in pharmacovigilance or in processing individual case safety reports (ICSRs).

For every role: a scientific or clinical degree, knowledge of EU/EEA pharmacovigilance legislation, MS Office and, for senior roles, good English.
